You wake up as an Orphan

No memories, no name, and a reflection that will not stop talking to you. That is the whole of your character creation.

What the Refuge changed

  • Every character starts with Warp Portal and Teleport level 1 as core skills. You do not have to earn mobility.
  • Heal and Hiding are capped at level 1 and rebuilt. Heal scales off your base level; Hiding is a fixed 2 seconds that works on bosses.
  • Manuals that granted those skills were reworked or removed, so guides written for the original world will point you at items that no longer do what they say.

Orphan

The Orphan is a cursed person. Every Orphan carries The Mark with them. Orphans create a Shadow when they die in specific places where negative energy is gathered, since Shadows attempt to explode when they die, they are very dangerous to society. For this reason, most people don't like having Orphans near them.

Orphans don't have access to the original jobs due to the risk they bring. Only the Thief, and in turn, Assassin and Rogue guilds accept Orphans, that's because those guilds would always be seen as outsiders by the common people, so they embraced these new outcasts from society.

Some alternative associations also seem to be taking Orphans...

An Orphan can also become a Merchant, as long as it sticks to the rules of the Merchant Association of Alberta and refrain from training and stick to a peaceful life of markets and trading.

Extra information

  • Orphans have this name because when a person receives The Mark, their whole family supposedly dies or vanishes. Nobody is fully sure what happens.
  • Available Weapons: Daggers
  • When an Orphan gets to Lv99 without dying, they receive +10 bonus to all stats. They also cannot job change anymore if they do this.

Orphan Dream

Being awakened as Orphan in a strange place, having no recollection of your Memories, yet Reflection is calling to you. Will your story be different then others, or will you follow the path of who you once was?

Now you as a player have an option of choosing Rates and remembering your name by speaking with Reflection.

The Orphanage

The hub. Every service you need before level 50 is in one building, and the Refuge gave the whole place a visual pass.

The Orphanage

The Orphanage is the best place for an Orphan to be in New-Midgard. A safe house born from the wishes of people to protect those who are less fortunated, and cursed.

It's a community effort to ensure the Orphanage continues to grow and provide better support for its people.

No matter how hard the path is, having a place to call home will always be something every orphan will have... as long as they take care of it.

Orphanage Level

The Orphanage grows with donations from players. Specific services and features will be available or expanded when the orphanage reaches higher levels.

Donating also rewards players with Donation Points that can be exchanged for costumes and rewards with some NPCs.

There are 3 ways to make the donations to the Orphanage:

When the orphanage levels up, EVERY CHARACTER currently online receives +50% EXP for 30 minutes. This will be broadcast and the Orphanage music will change briefly.

The money required and benefits for each level can be seen below.

  • Lucy Guile, the Healer, provides buffs at the cost of 5.000z.
  • This also rewards the player with 1 Donation Point and adds 1.000z to the Orphanage Funds.
  • Amadeus Windsor, the founder, takes direct donations of 10.000z, 50.000z and 100.000z.
  • This also rewards players with 10, 50 or 100 Donation Points.
  • Server Events, such as a monster invasion in a city.
  • If the players successfully save the city, a donation from the city might be made to the Orphanage, depending on how well players did.

Kafra Alysia

The kafra that works specifically for the orphanage. She can save your respawn point, access your storage, refill elixirs and teleport you.

  • The storage access here is always free.
  • Kafra Alysia has a minor quest associated with her.
  • Teleports with Alysia always cost 100z for Morroc and 500z for anywhere else.
  • Her teleports are always the cheapest option compared to city kafras.
  • Kafra Alysia teleport variety improves every 20 orphanage levels. She also has unique teleport destinations.

Healer

Lucy Guile takes cares of the Orphans, at no cost. She might seem cold but she's nice.

You can also pay her 5.000z for a temporary buff. You will receive all buffs already available

Duration of Buff is 5 minutes + 1 minute per orphanage level.

  • Magnificat, increased SP recovery, Orphanage Level 1 or higher.
  • Weapon Revest, prevents breaking and removal of weapon, Orphanage Level 25 or higher.
  • Job Growth, increases job experience gained by 10%, Orphanage Level 50 or higher.
  • Base Growth, increases base experience gained by 10%, Orphanage Level 100.

Kitchen

Mary Cenia is the Headchef of the kitchen, she can be a bit rough sometimes, but has a heart of gold.

You can buy food from her at a fixed price, regardless of the type or grade.

Food buffs don't stack. You can only have one stat food effect on the player [+1~10 and +20] .

Duration of Buff is 5 minutes + 1 minute per orphanage level.

  • Food can usually be bought in different cities around the world. Usually ranging from +2 to +7, as long as you find the Pub/Restaurant in them.
  • Price for food around the world is always more expensive than the Orphanage.
  • Every 10 Orphanage levels, the food quality increases by +1 stat point, up to +10 of a stat at Orphanage Level 90.

Memory Fountain

A fountain that you can see the reflection of your life. Allows you to reset your stats and skills.

  • The fountain reflects who you are now.
  • Price for resets is 500z per base level.
  • Price is reduced by 1% per orphanage level, making the fountain free at Orphanage Level 100.
  • There's no restrictions on how often the fountain can be used.

3 Smith Brothers

The 3 Howards take care of refines, weapons and armors. They sell equipment for Orphans at convenient prices.

  • The older brother refines equipment and sells ores.
  • The middle brother sells weapons.
  • the younger brother sells armors.
  • The quality of ores, weapons and armors improves every 10 orphanage levels, up to orphanage level 50.
  • very rare ores can be bought for HIGH prices at higher levels.

Card Remover

This fancy guy removes cards from any equipment.

  • There is no chance of failure.
  • The card remover requires Orphanage Level 25 to start his work.

Happy Girl and Big Dude

These two can provide different visuals to players.

  • Happy Girl can sell hats directly to players, at low prices (mostly).
  • She also sells zodiac Diadems, that provide reasonable bonuses, but have no slot.
  • Happy Girl also sells boxes containing a random SLOTTED hat of the same type above
  • Zodiac crowns are also obtained from boxes with a random hat inside. They are slotted variations of the diadems, and their bonuses are refine-based.
  • You can also get costume versions of all those items with her by spending Donation Points.
  • Big Dude provides garment costumes at very high amounts of Donation Points.
  • Big Dude sells garments when the Orphanage Level is 50 or higher.

Job Changers

A variety of places can start your job change around the orphanage.

(Jester, Vagabond and Raider jobs have other starting locations.)

  • The Merchant Representative can turn any Level 1/1 Orphan into a merchant permanently.
  • Lydia, the Thief can provide job level 10 Orphans with a quest to become a thief.
  • Mrs. Sorin can start Orphans on the path of the Trickster
  • A broken clock in the orphanage can bring back memories needed by a Illusionist.
  • A serious man is looking for strong orphans to start training as a Judge.
  • The Unchained room allows people ready to break their limits to push their jobs to the limit.

Wandering Minstrel

The Wandering minstrel will always provide updated information about the world to Orphans.

  • As patches and updates are made, the Minstrel will provide a small lore-friendly block of information about the new content.
  • It also provides overall information about the world.

Orphanage Level prices

Zeny required is a community goal. It can be donated from any player, at any time.

First 30 Levels:

  • Any Zeny donated above the required amount is LOST
  • Lucy the healer will never increase the level of the Orphanage, only an Amadeus Windsor donation will increase the level.
  • The donation amounts from Lucy the Healer can only help with overall progress.
  • Zeny required currently can be checked by asking for progress from Amadeus Windsor.
  • Zeny needed increases by 10.000z per current level. The funds go back to zero every level.
  • Some events and community achievements ingame might provide a boost to orphanage level progress, such as saving a city from an invasion
  • There's no limitation on how fast the Orphanage can level, theoretically, players can get it to Lv100 in a single day, with no limitations or daily stuff.

Your first job change

Ten job levels for a normal job, fifteen for an Expert one, twenty-five if you want to be a Mimic. The basement under the pyramids is where you get them.

First Job Change

Upon finishing tutorial you will be either 4/2 base/job level or 6/2 base/job level, based on rates chosen. You should get all basic stats VIT/DEX/INT to 10. then proceed on leveling main damaging stat to 30. Don't forget to put points into VIT/INT for max HP/SP and SP regen, and DEX for HIT if you miss a lot.

You can go down to Morroc Basement to get early levels for job change. While killing monsters in basement you can get your first normal gear and shadow set.

Level here until you get 10/10[Normal Jobs] or 15/10[Expert Jobs](except mimic he needs 25/10) for first Job Change.

Then proceed on changing jobs to:

Normal Jobs:

Thief - The Default Class in PRM. Has variety of skills, from melee/magic/ranged and passive to increase your Resource management or stat increases such as HP/SP/Flee.

Raider - Physical Bruiser Class. Unique job that focuses on using spears, dealing average damage and high survivability.

Trickster - Physical Bruiser leech based class. has inbuilt Leech from using Scythes. Fire Endow/AutoGuard and 1-2-3 combo playstyle.

Jester - Hybrid Physical/Magical class. Uses Unique Decks of cards that gives different effects depending on suit used. High ASPD limit. Burst and Spam Skills. and strong finisher that consumes HP to deal damage.

Ronin - Physical DPS Class. Excells on High Evade and burst damage with combo skills. Way of the Blade buff increases damage dealt with skills, but lost on taking any damage. Blade Skill Deals Melee damage in combo style. Void Dash skill deals Ranged damage by dashing behind target dealing delayed damage.

Expert Jobs:

Vagabond - Physical DPS class. Deals high dps, with low survivability. Has All element Endows with "Seven Winds". Passsives that increase ASPD/ATK and Flee. Frenzy that sets your APSD to 193 with increased ATK, and disables HP regenration.

Judge - Ranged DPS Class. Has burst damage with skills. High Mobility with Vault skill. Seals Management enables/boosts skills. Has access to variety of Revolvers made in Judge HQ

Dark Knight - Melee/Magic DPS/Bruiser Class. Harvest Buff Removes Regen, but increases Skill damage based on missing health. Black Heart gives 100% leech chance with 10% amount.

Peacekeeper - Melee/Ranged Class. Combo Style Character that has continuous combo gameplay. Damage scaled on dex, and other stats while under crash trinket effect. Trinkets can grant unique effects from Stat Scaling/Buffs/Debuffs.

Illusionist - Magic based Summoner class. Can summon Kimi to assist caster in combat. Uses Unique Gloves to change effect on Real Magic Attack. Grand Summoning can summon up to 5 monster to fight alongside Illusionist.

Mimic - 4fun Class. Can cast all 150 skills in the game depending on RNG with Abracadabra. Plagiarism can Save skill from other classes that he can use manually.

Getting Started

Upon Changing to your first Job. You will get "Weapon Voucher" that can be acquired from achievements tab, that can be exchanged for starting job weapon at Ornstein NPC located in backrooms on first floor. Choose weapon depending on what build you are going. it won't last for long time. Expert Jobs such as Judge and Peacekeeper receieves unique Accessory that gives +10% exp/ASPD +3/SP +10 until base level 30.

Now you will probably want to get some job levels. You can level in upper levels of Morroc Pyramids, if its too hard. you can always go back to basement until you level your damaging skills.

After you get your basic skills. its time to finally start the journey to level 130. it won't be an easy road. But remember PRM is Marathon, not a sprint.

Effective Exp from monsters counts by +/- 15 level range, but leveling spots will be listed with preferable player level and +5 of monsters level, Leveling spots will depend on your job/gear and stats, if its too hard, you can always level in easier spot or use reset fountain and spend points in stats that you missing: HP/SP/Damage/Cast Time and etc..

"Insect Society": 10 ~ 20. Even tho this dungeon considered low level dungeon. It can be quite rippy for certain jobs and while having low hp and hard DEF. Go here mostly for items.

Items to get: Insect Wings - Movement speed garment. Scaraba Tower - AutoGuard 10 shield with great defences. Quick Swarming shadow set for Variable cast reduction. Carapace Armor For melee classes. Dark Tights for ranged classes. Mother's nightmare ETC items for Insect society Scholar Accessory exchange.

"Prontera culvert F2": 14 ~ 34. Mega Thief Bug/Fulbuk. Good leveling spot especially if you have AoE.

Items to get: Running shoes - high base movement speed shoes, look for Move speed random option. Golden Buckler - good sheild with Regen.

"Geffen Cave entrance map": 20 ~ 35. Goblins.

Items to get: Wooden Mail, Gives large HP bonus for max SP cost, great for Expert Jobs since their hp pool is low, if you can sustain SP.

Where to level, all the way to 130

The single most useful thing the community ever wrote down: which map, at which level, and what to take away from it.

Read this before you follow a level range

  • Monsters deal more damage than they used to and healing is slower, so a spot that was comfortable in the original may not be.
  • Experience is slightly slower than the original's, but the curve is more rewarding.
  • Drop-rate gear no longer exists, so there is nothing to farm before you farm.

Journey Continues

Faceworm Nest: 53 ~ 74. Best Leveling place in Level range, High EXP from every monster. Huge density. It's so over

After level 100, game becomes slightly harder, you will start having issues in certain dungeons, and on certain jobs, but its not a reason to stop.

Now is good time to get more powerful gear, and start refining, slotting cards, looking for costumes = the cooler you look, the more damage you deal, and that's facts.

We're So Back!

After achieving level 100, you will spend most of the time getting new and better gear,refining, trying out builds, exploring various locations, and getting jobs for new class skills.

Most of the dungeons will increase in difficulty, meaning without upgrades it will be now tough to level.

Leveling Spots:

Nameless Island: 95 ~ 105. Good place to level early on after getting lvl 100.

Items to get: Job runes from "lost rune coffer". Manuals from banshee. Mob cards for for lower headgear. Invoker's Ring from BIshop MVP.

Verus Fields: 100 ~ 110. Monsters here doesn't give Base level EXP. but they give Huge amounts of Job EXP. if you are missing on some skill points. its great place to grind for Job levels without changing spots, since monsters doesn't give Base level EXP.

Items to get: Excellion set.

''Old Glast Heim Distortion: 105 ~ 115". Monsters deal massive amounts of damage. Spectre Raydrics have ghost 4 so neutral attacks aren't effective.

Level 100 and the Bifrost Mirror

Distortion dungeons are the first content gate that asks you to prepare rather than just arrive.

What the Orb?

After you acquired lvl 100, and changed to final job, now is the time to start exploring "Distortion Dungeons". A unique dungeons ,that offers more items to farm, with slight difficulty increase, depending on distortion of course, it can vary from debuffing/buffing player and monsters while in dungeon, enables free pvp for every player inside, draining full exp on death or just disabling resurrection.

To enter such dungeons player would need "Bifrost Mirror", following the guide will help you find both Bifrost shard, and 4 eyes of the sons of Odin. Each socketed eye allows traveling in time, be it either past, future, or completly new timeline.

I hope you saved a lot of zeny for Sage's service.

  • Socketing all 4 eyes into the bifrost shard, gives access to distortion dungeon for whole account, you only need to finish bifrost mirror once.
  • Don't rush by socketing all the eyes. get first 2 eyes for 100-120 level Distortions, socket missing eyes once you have spare zeny or need Distortion entry.

A Piece of Asgard in New-Midgard

The Bifrost Mirror Shard is a piece of equipment that cannot be equipped, sold, traded, dropped or stored, being 100% Character Bound.

This item is required to access the Distortion Dungeons, as it can reflect the light seen on the blue orbs that contains the distortion to be accessed. The Mirror by itself is useless, but the Eyes of the 4 Sons of Odin can power it up in order to unlock access to different distortions.

Upon fully completing Bifrost Mirror on any character , gives access to all distortion on whole account regardless of having Biforst Mirror in inventory.

How to Obtain

The Mirror is not dropped, instead its location can be described as follows:

Where Satan Morroc was slain, the Mirror shall bring a new light to anyone who seeks to find a different path.

When the Mirror is obtained, the player can begin working on developing it, as the Mirror itself has no powers.

The Mirror can be found in moc_fild14 - 345,196

The 4 Jewels

The 4 Jewels of the sons of Odin are spread around the world. they can be found in 4 different regions:

The sons of Odin walked the land and each was visiting one of the nations: Rune-Midgard, Arunafeltz, Schwarzwald and Niflheim.

The eyes must be slotted in a fixed order, only the sages in Dicastes Diel can add them.

  • First Eye: pay_fild06 - 103,222
  • Second Eye: hu_fild06 - 188,230
  • Third Eye: ra_fild03 - 214,267
  • Fourth Eye: Niflheim - 289,262

Slotting the Eyes

The Sages in Dicastes Diel are dressed according to their expertise based on their colors. The distortions available for each can be found on the Distortion Dungeons Page.

The Eyes on a Bifrost Mirror Shard can be seen like the slots of cards on an equipment by checking the Mirror in the inventory.

This is considered a requirement for any distortion access, and once done, the character permanently unlocks that access. Regardless if the player loses a fully upgraded Mirror, they retain permanent access to those.

The Master of Vidar slots the first Jewel. The price to add the Eye of Vidar is 75.000z

This enables access to past distortions, they took place in the past and are part of history.

The Master of Vali slots the second Jewel. The price to add the Eye of Vali is 100.000z

This enables access to future distortions, they indicate a situation that may happen someday and must be avoided.

The Master of Thor slots the third Jewel. The price to add the Eye of Thor is 150.000z

This enables access to paradox distortions, they are unique places that happen in different timelines or circumstances to our world.

The Master of Balder slots the fourth Jewel. The price to add the Eye of Balder is 350.000z

This enables access to end of the world distortions, they take place in apocalyptic scenarios where all hope is lost and a great evil roams the land freely.

130, 150, and what comes after

One hundred and thirty is the soft cap. One hundred and fifty is a challenge rather than a goal.

Is this the End?

After getting level 124 stat gained are heavily decreased, once you achieve Level 130 it is known as "Soft-Cap" meaning after this point no new gear will appear, its not required to level past this point. You still can level up up to level 150, after level 130 exp needed to level up are increased massively, fewer places to level, every death will remove 5% exp which can delete hours of leveling on higher levels.

level 150 or "Hard Cap", in itself is a challenge, after 145 level you won't gain any more stat points.

What to do now?. Now it depends on you how to taste the End-Game, will you try to conquer harderst dungeons in the game? Explore world for hidden quests? Grind for your character upgrades, to be the best one on the server?
