== For the King ==
The [[Judge]] is a unique job in Return to Morroc and one of the expert jobs. It's defining trait is the access to the Headquarters, an area of Prontera Castle exclusive to Judges.

This area has all the NPCs a player may need for basic combat, progression lore and even secret skills.

Visit [[Combat Reworked]] for details on how the combat works and it's dynamics.

== Location ==
The Headquarters are located behind the Throne Room in Prontera Castle. An Orphan will be allowed access to this area after a certain point in their quest in order to finish their job change. The area can only be accessed by Judges under any circumstance.

== Notable People ==
* '''Catherine Gaebolg''', Widow to King Tristram III is the ceremonial leader of the Judges and responsible for the job change.
* '''Quartermaster''' can sell supplies and can also remove cards specifically from revolvers.
* '''Friedrich Heine''' captain of inner affair of the Judges, can provide titles and promotions for special feats. ('''Planned feature, not available''')
* '''Lovejoy''' captain of deployment missions of the Judges, can provide good information about how to obtain different weapons and ammo... according to your level.
* '''Elena Bolkova''' Daughter of a famous gunsmith, is looking for a strong Judge who mastered multiple weapons to help her with an important issue, found outside HQ, around the castle.
* '''Regional Judges''' provide quests and progression for multiple weapons.

== Royal Arms Crafting ==
A Judge has multiple weapons they must carry at all times. All judge weapons are character-bound and unique. They never have weapon random modifiers but have strong innate effects.

To craft a revolver, the Judge usually requires specific materials requested by someone. There are also unique revolvers that can be rewarded for specific requirements not in the Headquarters.
