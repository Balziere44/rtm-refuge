== Specializations ==
They are a unique part of the progression of the [[Thief]] tree. Specializations provide a unique step in the progression for them.

For anyone unfamiliar, they are similar to the old Rebirth Jobs, except that they just expand your job capabilities, they don't reset it.

== Details for Specializations ==
To choose a specialization:

* Assassin or Rogue Job Level 50
* Any Base Level
* Talk to a Master in the Desert Guild
* You will receive a free skill reset. Your Job Level will continue from 50 but max job level will be expanded to 70.
* You can use skill points freely between Assassin/Rogue or the Specialization skills.
* Once you're Job Level 70, you can advance to the final job.

There are 8 Specializations Available:

'''Assassin'''

* Blade Dancer. Specialization focused on speed and dual wielding. Evolves into [[Night Raven]].
* Executioner. Specialization focused on criticals and katars. Evolves into [[Gravekeeper]].
* Scoundrel. Specialization focused on poisons and axes. Evolves into [[Black Plague]].
* Shinobi. Specialization focused on covering all aspects. Evolves into [[Satsujin]].

'''Rogue'''

* Duelist. Specialization focused on preventing and countering damage. Evolves into [[Kingslayer]].
* Saboteur. Specialization focused on traps and bombs. Evolves into [[Blast Juggler]].
* Deadeye. Specialization focused on preparation and bows. Evolves into [[Sinner]].
* Mystic. Specialization focused on magic and elements. Evolves into [[Arcane Master]].
