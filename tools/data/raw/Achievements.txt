Return to Morroc features custom Achievements and Achievement Rewards!

Remember to check the tab Achievement Challenges tab [[File:AchievCup.gif|AchievCup]], some achievements can help you in the early game by giving freebies!

* Many Achievements are granted automatically on completion, some are achieved by talking to NPCs and More!

* There are Achievements which can help you figure out where to progress next!

* Some achievements have been grouped together based on how they are earned. Their original category has also been listed in parentheses.
