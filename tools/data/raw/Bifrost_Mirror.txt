== A Piece of Asgard in New-Midgard ==
The '''Bifrost Mirror Shard''' is a piece of equipment that cannot be equipped, sold, traded, dropped or stored, being 100% Character Bound.

This item is required to access the [[Distortion Dungeons]], as it can reflect the light seen on the blue orbs that contains the distortion to be accessed. The Mirror by itself is useless, but the Eyes of the 4 Sons of Odin can power it up in order to unlock access to different distortions.

'''Upon fully completing Bifrost Mirror on any character , gives access to all distortion on whole account regardless of having Biforst Mirror in inventory.'''

== How to Obtain ==
[[File:Map1.png|thumb|right|Bifrost Mirror location|150px|Bifrost Mirror location]]
The Mirror is not dropped, instead its location can be described as follows:

'''Where Satan Morroc was slain, the Mirror shall bring a new light to anyone who seeks to find a different path.'''

When the Mirror is obtained, the player can begin working on developing it, as the Mirror itself has no powers.

'''The Mirror can be found in moc_fild14 - 345,196'''

== The 4 Jewels ==
The '''4 Jewels of the sons of Odin''' are spread around the world. they can be found in 4 different regions:

'''The sons of Odin walked the land and each was visiting one of the nations: Rune-Midgard, Arunafeltz, Schwarzwald and Niflheim.'''

The eyes must be slotted in a fixed order, only the sages in Dicastes Diel can add them.

* '''First Eye:''' pay_fild06 - 103,222
* '''Second Eye:''' hu_fild06 - 188,230
* '''Third Eye:''' ra_fild03 - 214,267
* '''Fourth Eye:''' Niflheim - 289,262
[[File:Map2.png|thumb|4 Eyes locations|250px|4 Eyes locations]]

== Slotting the Eyes ==
The Sages in Dicastes Diel are dressed according to their expertise based on their colors. The distortions available for each can be found on the [[Distortion Dungeons]] Page.

The Eyes on a Bifrost Mirror Shard can be seen like the slots of cards on an equipment by checking the Mirror in the inventory.

This is considered a requirement for any distortion access, and once done, the character permanently unlocks that access. Regardless if the player loses a fully upgraded Mirror, they retain permanent access to those.

'''The Master of Vidar slots the first Jewel. The price to add the Eye of Vidar is 75.000z'''

This enables access to past distortions, they took place in the past and are part of history.

'''The Master of Vali slots the second Jewel. The price to add the Eye of Vali is 100.000z'''

This enables access to future distortions, they indicate a situation that may happen someday and must be avoided.

'''The Master of Thor slots the third Jewel. The price to add the Eye of Thor is 150.000z'''

This enables access to paradox distortions, they are unique places that happen in different timelines or circumstances to our world.

'''The Master of Balder slots the fourth Jewel. The price to add the Eye of Balder is 350.000z'''

This enables access to end of the world distortions, they take place in apocalyptic scenarios where all hope is lost and a great evil roams the land freely.
