Everyone has a story, even your character!
The main story quest gives you a chance to figure out all the questions you may have about who you are, what's the mark, who are the Orphans and much more!
'''It's completely optional but does have a few rewards.'''

=== Main Story Progression ===

The main story is comprised of 12 Quests + Tutorial Introduction. With 8 Instances in total.
'''Note that only the tutorial and chapters 1-5 of the main story quest are currently available. The remainder will be implemented with upcoming patches.'''
