= [[File:Legend.gif|25px|link=]]Unmatched Warrior =

The Legend is a natural evolution for the best Vagabonds, it increases its auto attack potentials to the limit.
It has very limited alternate tools and requires smart play to succeed.

'''Greatest changes from Vagabond:'''
* Dual wielding

* Access to manageable buffs

* Safety wall.

'''Weapons:''' Daggers.

==New Mechanics==
The Legend, like the Vagabond, learns the full skill tree eventually. This ensures it always has full access to its kit, as simple as it may be.
A good Legend will know how to take the most advantage from its damage buff '''Harness Magic''', as it can convert MATK and INT into more auto attack damage, making even some magic-bonus weapons useful under the right circumstances.
As it still relies on auto attacks, its a simple job mechanically, but can be very complex to correctly balance and extract the most both in damage and in survival.
Safety Wall comes at the very end of the skill tree, but it's the best short-range protection available for a party due to how flexible it is.
