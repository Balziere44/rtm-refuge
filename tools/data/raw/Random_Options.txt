== Rarity System & Rerolling ==

We are introducing two brand new systems made from scratch that allow any piece of equipment, including shadow gear, runes, and manuals, to have a rarity.
<div style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px; text-align: center;">
  https://media.tenor.com/4SP8JrTcZdYAAAAM/despair.gif
  <div style="font-size: 12px; color: #666;">When no Doomed drops... 🤕</div>
</div>

Every equipment that drops from monsters will roll a chance to have a rarity.

Each rarity will give a bonus to the value of the equipment options.


=== Rarity Levels ===
Equipment items can drop with different rarity levels, each with their own drop rates:

{| class="wikitable"
! Rank !! Visual !! Name !! Drop Rate !! Bonus Range
|-
| No Rank || - || Common || - || No changes
|-
| Rank D || [[File:Forsaken.png|link=https://wiki.echoesofmorroc.com/images/6/67/Forsaken.png?20250214230836]] || Forsaken || 40% || 20% - 120% of base max
|-
| Rank C || [[File:Haunted.PNG|link=https://wiki.echoesofmorroc.com/images/e/e8/Haunted.PNG?20250214230824]] || Haunted || 25% || 50% - 150% of base max
|-
| Rank B || [[File:Cursed.PNG|link=https://wiki.echoesofmorroc.com/images/d/d9/Cursed.PNG]] || Cursed || 8% || 80% - 180% of base max
|-
| Rank A || [[File:Doomed.PNG|link=https://wiki.echoesofmorroc.com/images/0/08/Doomed.PNG?20250214230800]] || Doomed || 0.2% || 110% - 200% of base max
|}

==== Important Notes ====
* These rarity levels only apply to equipment items
* Ammunition and costume items are excluded from the rarity system
* The interaction with rarity is based on the '''max possible random bonus''' of the item
* Higher grades allow for exceeding normal random bonus limitations

For instance, if you have a '''Haunted Knife''', the ''All Magical DMG'' bonus could exceed the usual 10% max value.

=== Reroll System ===
On top of the rarity system, which increases the RNG elements of an item, we introduce the reroll system to improve the quality of life.

We will have '''ten types of Orbs''', each granting a form of item modification (and inventory).


Every monster has a chance to drop an orb, but some orbs drop only from MVPs or can be obtained from the '''World Boss Shop'''.

The drop chance increases depending on the mob tier: 30, 75, 100, 130
