The **Paradox system** introduces a clear separation between different gameplay environments, designed to support **seasonal content** and alternative progression paths without affecting the main game.

At its core, Paradox exists to ensure that progress made in one environment cannot be carried into another. This keeps each experience fair, self-contained, and balanced over time.

== Realm separation ==
Characters and items belong to a specific realm.  Interactions are only allowed when both sides belong to the same realm.

In practice, this means that Paradox and Normal gameplay do not mix.  Items, zeny, and progression obtained in Paradox cannot be transferred to Normal characters, and vice versa.

This separation helps prevent indirect advantages, same-account transfers, and unintended shortcuts between different progression environments.

== What gets blocked ==
To preserve this separation, interactions across realms are restricted.  When a mismatch is detected, the action is simply not allowed.

This includes, but is not limited to:

* Trading items or zeny
* Player shops (vending and buying stores)
* Storage and guild storage access
* Mail and auctions
* Party and guild interactions
* Using shortcuts or portals created by characters from another realm

These restrictions apply consistently and are not meant to be bypassed during normal gameplay.

== Paradox and Seasons ==
Paradox is closely tied to the concept of **Seasons**.

Each season represents a fresh, self-contained experience where players can start over, explore new mechanics, and progress through exclusive content.

Paradox characters are the foundation of seasonal gameplay.

== Seasonal questlines ==
Each Paradox season features its own dedicated questlines.  These quests are designed to introduce the seasonal theme, guide progression, and encourage exploration of season-specific mechanics.

Seasonal questlines are exclusive to Paradox characters and do not interact with Normal progression.

== Seasonal tickets & rewards ==
Seasons use a separate reward system based on **Seasonal Tickets**.

These tickets are earned through Paradox gameplay and can be exchanged for seasonal rewards, cosmetics, and other exclusive items tied to that season.

Seasonal rewards are designed to remain within the Paradox ecosystem and do not affect the Normal economy.

== Design philosophy ==
Paradox is not meant to replace the Normal game.  It exists as an optional, parallel experience that allows for experimentation, fresh progression, and seasonal content.

By keeping realms isolated, the server can safely introduce new ideas, mechanics, and progression models while preserving the integrity of the core game.
