== <span id="Basic_doesn.27t_mean_Bad"></span><span id="Basic_doesn't_mean_Bad" class="mw-headline">Basic doesn't mean Bad</span> ==

The Equipment sold by NPCs in Return to Morroc don't always mean they are useless. They can be a good thing to get if you need to fill any remaining slots or needs a small bump in power.

Field monsters may sometimes have unique drops, but '''they are always guaranteed to drop a NPC equipment with random mods and +1 Slot at a low chance'''.

== <span id="Shop_Types" class="mw-headline">Shop Types</span> ==

The kind of equipment you can find in each shop is determined by their region, each one with a corresponding shop type:

'''Midgard Shop''': Can be found in Prontera, Izlude, Morroc, Payon, Alberta, Aldebaran, Comodo and Geffen.

* Midgard Items usually consist of minor, but impactful bonuses such as ASPD or Cast Time.

'''Schwarzwald Shop''': Can be found in Yuno, Hugel, Einbroch, Einbech and Lighthalzen.

* Schwarzwald Items usually consist of combat and immunity bonuses.

'''Arunafeltz Shop''': Can be found in Rachel and Veins.

* Arunafeltz Items usually consist of situational bonuses that can help in specific scenarios.

'''Island Shop''': Can be found in Dewata, Amatsu, Ayothaya, Malaya and Moscovia.

* Island Items usually have refine based bonuses that increase combat consistency such as SP consumption or critical rates.

'''Tribal Shop''': Can be found in Umbala, Mora and Nomad Village.

* Trival Items consist of negative status effects, elements and minor offensive bonuses.

'''Niflheim Shop''': Can be found in Niflheim and Sage's Palace: Dicastes Diel.

* They are overly expensive items that provide high bonuses with large downsides. They are situational but very unique.
