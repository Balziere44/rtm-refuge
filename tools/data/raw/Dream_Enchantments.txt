= <span id="Dream Enchants" class="mw-headline">Dream Enchants</span> =

Dream enchants are unqiue enchantments on certain gear.
For items to be enchanted you need specific items that have "Dream Enchant Avalible" option.

Items such as:

"Sky Garden exchange".

"Rachel Nightmare Distortion" gear.

Some "Judge" Weapons.

And more.

"Weaver of Dreams" Npc can give enchantments to desired items.

'''Success rate of enchant are 10% + "10%xRefine amount" = Total chance.'''

'''Cost of enchantments are 100 Dream Shards'''.

'''Reseting enchantments cost 500 Distortion Shards'''.

"Dream Box" can trade Distortion Essence for Dream gear set and Dream Pouches, than contains from 1~10 Dream Shards each.
