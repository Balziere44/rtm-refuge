= <span id="HATred_Dude" class="mw-headline">HATred Dude</span> =

<div class="floatright">

</div>
The '''HATred Dude''' is well known around the world as a very angry guy who hates all monsters.

When a player finds him, he will offer to give one of his hats in exchange for items dropped from monsters of that region or dungeon.

Each HATred quest can only be completed '''ONCE''' per character, however the hats are fully tradeable, and can also be stored.

== <span id="HATred_Points" class="mw-headline">HATred Points</span> ==

Whenever a player finishes a '''HATred quest''' they will be awarded a HATred Point. To check how many points you have, just speak with the HATred Dude anywhere in the world and he will let you know how many quests you have finished. The purpose of the HATred Points is unknown.

Below is a '''full list''' of all HATred Quests.

== <span id="Location" class="mw-headline">Location</span> ==

The location of the HATred dude is '''never''' indicated in any minimap or mentioned by any NPC. It requires either word of mouth or exploration to find him in every location.

He can be identified by his unique appearance as well as a banner over his head reading &quot;HATred Quest&quot; with a Headgear icon.

'''PS: Distortion Dungeons don't have the HATred Dude as all of them drop Distortion Essence at varied rates as regular loot.'''
