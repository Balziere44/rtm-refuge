<p>The Thief is a resourceful fighter, it can wield multiple weapon types and evolve into multiple paths.</p>

<p>Over the years, the Thief guild worked hard not only to expand its skill set to allow multiple new training paths, but to fully utilize the potential of Orphans to achieve greater advances.</p>

<p>Thief no longer has access to Double Attack, Double Attack is an innate bonus depending on the Daggers.</p>

==Skills==

<p>The Thief is a job class that can specialize in any kind of damage in the game. It also can wield multiple weapons to achieve this.
Max Job Level for Thief is 50. A Thief can become either an Assassin, a Rogue or Unchain its limits.</p>
