= <span id="New_Exploration" class="mw-headline">New Exploration</span> =

Return to Morroc provides a whole new world to explore. It's a familiar but heavily changed place, all done in a way to make exploration rewarding, unique and varied.


== <span id="Regions" class="mw-headline">Regions</span> ==

Regions of Midgard are vastly changed compared to original places. Some areas might be familiar to some, but the way they work may be completely changed. Below is a full breakdown of all major field regions.

Regions never spawn a Shadow when a player dies.

== <span id="Dungeons" class="mw-headline">Dungeons</span> ==

Dungeons are regular places to hunt for items, cards and level up. They are harder than most fields but always carry considerable and unique rewards. Dungeons are NOT instance-based. They are open maps that can be explored as much as needed.

They also have a Ranking that dictates how hard a dungeon is, the rank also dictates what shadows will spawn for the [https://web.archive.org/web/20230822235444/https://returntomorroc.com/wiki/index.php/Shadow_System Shadow System].
