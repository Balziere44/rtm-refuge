'''Jesters''' are medium ranged card fighters that deal quick and constant damage while burning through their huge SP pool.

The Jester core-mechanic is ''weapon swapping'', as it can't recover SP naturally while fighting with Deck cards, needing to swap into a Shuffling Deck to recover SP in many different ways.

Its skills are a mix of physical and magical damage, with multiple combo interactions, allowing for creative and adaptive gameplay.

The Quest necessary for becoming a Jester can be found at the [[Job Quests]] page under the Unique Tree section.

* '''Pros:''' Fast skill use, easy to play, multiple playstyles and builds, massive SP Pool.

* '''Cons:''' Weapon swapping can be hard to master, medium ranged, low defense and defensive options.

* '''Weapon:[[File:DeckIcon.png|24x24px|link=DeckIcon]] =''' Decks, Shuffling Decks
* '''Main Stats:''' LUK, INT, DEX

== <span id="Identity" class="mw-headline">Identity</span> ==

As defining characteristics, the Jester class has:

* LUK and INT as core status for skill damage scaling.
* A two (or more) '''weapon-swap mechanic''', one mainly for recovering SP (the Joker or ''Shuffling-Deck'' cards) and other for dealing damage (the Suits or ''deck'' cards).
* ASPD-Based animation for its skills (which have short to zero cast time).
* A very large SP pool, but with very high SP costs.
* Magic, Physical or Hybrid build options.
* Combo-based interactions.

This makes the Jester Class a “'''Rapid Fire Skill Caster, with coverage of both physical and magical damage, with an SP reloading mechanic on weapon swap.'''”

== <span id="Weapons" class="mw-headline">[[Weapons]]</span> ==

The Jester uses Cards ([[File:DeckIcon.png|24x24px|link=DeckIcon]]), which are divided in two types: the Deck and the Shuffling deck cards. Both types are '''two-handed''' and exclusive to the class.

==== <span id="Deck_.28or_Suits.29_Cards"></span><span id="Deck_(or_Suits)_Cards" class="mw-headline">Deck (or Suits) Cards</span> ====

Allows the use of spells, being the damage dealing card. They have an innate ''sp reduction'' passive, but also '''lock you out of natural SP regeneration'''.

Currently, there are four type of Deck cards:

♠ '''''Spades''''' (Balanced): provides a balanced amount of ATK and MATK, with options to increase damage of magic or physical skills and increased ''sp reduction''.

♣ '''''Clubs''''' (Physical Attack): for physical builds, provides a great amount of ATK and bonus for skills such as ''First Hand'' [[File:Firsthand.gif|26x26px|link=Firsthand]].

♥ '''''Hearts''''' (Magic Attack): for magical builds, provides a great amount of MATK and bonus for skills such as ''Instant Aces'' [[File:Instantaces.gif|26x26px|link=Instantaces]].

♦ '''''Diamonds''''' (Defensive): a more defensive option, provides bonus to defensive attributes.

==== <span id="Joker_.28or_Shuffling_Deck.29_Cards"></span><span id="Joker_(or_Shuffling_Deck)_Cards" class="mw-headline">Joker (or Shuffling Deck) Cards</span> ====

Joker Cards allow '''natural SP regeneration''' while also improving/creating other means of SP recovery. However, it '''disables the use of most offensive spells'''.

While technically not locking you out of defensive spells, such &quot;Heal&quot; and &quot;Hiding&quot;, using them with a Joker Card equipped '''will consume a large amount of SP'''.

== <span id="Gameplay" class="mw-headline">Gameplay</span> ==

Jesters have a great freedom of skill rotation options, both for magic and physical variants, based on '''combo or ability spam''' playstyles.

Nonetheless, due to its low defensive status and overall lack of defensive spells, a mastery (or mix) of both styles is recommended for succeding in different situations.

==== <span id="Offensive_skills" class="mw-headline">Offensive skills</span> ====

* Physical combos use as a common opener the '''First Hand''' [[File:Firsthand.gif|26x26px|link=Firsthand]], following up with Double Down [[File:Doubledown.gif|26x26px|link=Doubledown]] or Arcana of Destiny [[File:Arcanaofdestiny.gif|26x26px|link=Arcanaofdestiny]].
* Using '''Joker's Draw''' [[File:Jokerdraw.gif|26x26px|link=Jokerdraw]] (which require '''Stack Deck''') as it activates combo followup.
* As a desperation act, jesters can call the '''Curtain Call''' [[File:Curtaincall.gif|26x26px|link=Curtaincall]] to consume all HP (but 1) to deal heavy damage to a single target under a high risk of retaliation.
* '''Instant Aces''' [[File:Instantaces.gif|26x26px|link=Instantaces]] is a quick spell that can be spammed without combo interactions, providing a simple spam alternative.
* '''Royal Flush''' [[File:Royalflush.gif|26x26px|link=Royalflush]] can be used as a final combo skill for a heavy magic AoE against enemies.

==== <span id="Defensive_skills" class="mw-headline">Defensive skills</span> ====

* '''Deflect Blades'''[[File:Deflectblades.gif|26x26px|link=Deflectblades]] provides a damage reduction and retaliation against some enemy attacks. However, there are some ''risks'' to it due to the skill animation.
* '''Lady Luck's Kiss''' [[File:Ladyluckkiss.gif|26x26px|link=Ladyluckkiss]] is the only supportive spell available for Jesters, removing most ''Abnormal Status'', even on yourself.
