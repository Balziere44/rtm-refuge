= Sky Garden Exchange =

Sky Garden Is a custom dungeon with 3 layers of maps with normal mobs and MVP on 3rd Floor.

Every mob drops [[File:Einherjar Soul.GIF|24x24px|link=EinherjarSoul]] '''Einherjar Soul''', 100 of this item can be exchanged for [[File:Odin's blessing.GIF|24x24px|link=OdinBlessing]] '''Odin's Blessing'''.
Sarah Irene "MVP" and Avatar of Thor also drops [[File:Odin's blessing.GIF|24x24px|link=OdinBlessing]] '''Odin's Blessing'''.


[[File:Sky Garden Exchange.GIF|thumb|240px|File:Sky Garden Exchange.GIF|]]

To exchange [[File:Einherjar Soul.GIF|24x24px|link=EinherjarSoul]] '''Einherjar Soul''' for [[File:Odin's blessing.GIF|24x24px|link=OdinBlessing]] '''Odin's Blessing''' , Player would need to meet "Wish Maiden" at the end of the dungeon for both Blessing and Items exchange.



"'''Swordsman?'''" NPC can teleport player back to the entrance of the dungeon, if you wish to start over.
