==[[File:Prowler.gif]] Invisible Acrobat ==
The Prowler is a very evasive job, which uses the few tools it has available to create openings and overwhelm the targets with skills.

It has incredible dodging and damage negation methods making it extremely safe under skilled hands.

'''Best:''' High burst damage, amazing damage negation, small and tight skill kit.

'''Worst:''' High SP Cost, vulnerable to unavoidable damage, restricted left hand slot.

'''Weapons:''' Daggers, Prowler's Armguards

=== Gameplay Identity ===
The Prowler is a job that focuses on creating opportunities to instantly take down its targets or to dance around them avoiding hits, for this reason:

* They are extremely deadly while being hard to counter-react, as long as their SP remains, they will overwhelm any target with quick hits.

As Prowlers have very small skill kits, they are straightforward to build. Relying on their special passive skills and [[Shields#Prowler_Gear_list|Armguards]] to work in a multitude of ways.

The Thief  skill '''Backstab''' is the centerpiece of the Prowler's kit. With the boosts from the passives, this skill can activate Combo Ready and receive incredible power boosts, allowing a Prowler to quickly start any combat from any situation in an advantage, ready to follow up in a multitude of ways, usually with '''Shadowstab'''.

Another unique tool to the Prowler is the '''Rope Kunai''', a unique weapon that allows a Prowler to pull enemies close by using the '''Flying Knife''' skill.

=== Core Gameplay Loop ===
A key factor on the Prowler is it's easy access to Perfect Dodge, making attaining even 100% perfect dodge, an easy task for most builds. This may not negate every damage taken, but the almost complete negation of all auto attacks makes them very resistant and virtually unkillable if correctly played.

Most times, a Prowler will use Backstab to close-in on enemies and follow up with Shadowstab for as long as the Combo Ready window allows. This makes building both SP, SP regen and ASPD essential for the Prowler in order to not run out of energy for the barrage of skills.

Rope Kunai can be used to bring enemies that are out of reach, increasing the overall fighting speed. Whirling Kunai is a very strong, short range (but still ranged) damage skill that allows for heavy damage in an area, Even with a long cooldown, it's a reliable and strong tool to fit into rotations.

Macabre is a powerful buff that increases the overall fighting capacity of the Prowler for a duration by consuming HP, being a great risk-rewards mechanic to play around.
The multiple evasive skills of the Prowler can be used to greatly reduce damage for the specific cases where they are needed.
