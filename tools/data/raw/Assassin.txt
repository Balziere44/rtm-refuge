== Assassin ==
This job is the first on the "Power of Tradition" job tree. A tree focused on direct combat, it can dual wield multiple weapons.

The Assassin and Rogue guilds have united themselves in order to better allocate their resources and share training. With this, the Assassins had the opportunity to learn new ways of combat, while sticking to their traditions.

'''Weapons Available:''' Daggers, Swords, Axes, Katars

=== Skill Trees ===
The Assassin is a job class that can specialize in direct damage, critical damage, or magic damage. It can wield multiple weapons as dual wield. Max Job Level for Assassin is 50. An Assassin can choose one of 4 Specializations or Unchain its limits once it hits Base Level 99.
