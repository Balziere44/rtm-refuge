<p>Jobs are not only ways to play here, but also a unique identity of the player. The Job Change Quests are an essential part of this.</p>

= Thief Tree =
The Thief tree is the "regular progression" tree of the game. There are multiple job quests and paths to be taken. It's the perfect way to follow your destiny.

=== [[File:Thief.gif]]Thief Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Lydia the Thief, Orphanage

'''Required:''' Orphan Job Lv10

'''Objective:''' Pay the price and assist the guild by talking to Lydia on the Orphanage entrance

=== [[File:Prowler.gif]]Prowler Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Lighthalzen

'''Required:''' Thief Job Lv50

'''Objective:''' Use the Prowler Armguard and prove you can fight the strongest enemies no matter what.

=== [[File:Shadowseer.gif]]Shadowseer Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Niflheim

'''Required:''' Thief Job Lv50

'''Objective:''' Understand the path of Life and Death.

=== [[File:Assassin.gif]][[File:Rogue.gif]]Assassin/Rogue Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Sograt Desert Guild - moc_fild16

'''Required:''' Thief Job Lv50

'''Objective:''' Clear all the trials of the assassins, including basic knowledge, evasion and intuition to choose into become an Assassin or a Rogue.

'''Note:''' The Master at the top of the pyramid can help any thief with their Assassin and Rogue job quest.

=== [[File:Unchthief.gif]][[File:Unchassassin.gif]][[File:Unchrogue.gif]]Unchained Classes Quests ===

'''Start Location:''' Master Room, Orphanage

'''Required:''' Job Level 50 (Unchained Thief), Base Level 99, Job Level 50 (Unchained Assassin, Unchained Rogue, Phantom Thief)

'''Objective:''' A specific weapon with random options will be requested at a high refine level, bring the weapon to the master. The weapon will need to be at the EXACT refine level.

=== [[File:Bladedancer.gif]][[File:Scoundrel.gif]][[File:Shinobi.gif]][[File:Executioner.gif]][[File:Mystic.gif]][[File:Duelist.gif]][[File:Saboteur.gif]][[File:Deadeye.gif]]Specialization Quests ===

'''Start Location:''' Desert Guild, moc_fild16

'''Required:''' Assassin or Rogue Job Lv50

'''Objective:''' Talk to the master to learn the details about a specialization, the master will change you into the specialization if you wish.

'''Note:''' Entrance to the Desert Guild is on the center island, to the left-side/west. The Top of the temple is only for the Assassin and Rogue tests.

=== [[File:Nightraven.gif]]Night Raven Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Sky Brother, Rachel Fields

'''Required:''' Blade Dancer Job Lv70

'''Objective:''' Scout the area and prepare a escape route to save the people from the cultists. This Quest is shared with Scoundrel's quest, you'll work outside.

=== [[File:Blackplague.gif]]Black Plague Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Land Brother, Rachel Fields

'''Required:''' Scoundrel Job Lv70

'''Objective:''' Infiltrate the cultist base and help the people escape. This Quest is shared with Blade Dancer's quest, you'll work inside.

=== [[File:Satsujin.gif]]Satsujin Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Master Saito, Yuno

'''Required:''' Shinobi Job Lv70

'''Objective:''' Capture the mysterious fugitive and survive all its traps.

=== [[File:Gravekeeper.gif]]Gravekeeper Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Tomb Guardian, Pyramid Basement

'''Required:''' Executioner Job Lv70

'''Objective:''' Enter Loki's tomb and learn the hardships of the Assassin path.

=== [[File:Arcanemaster.gif]]Arcane Master Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Mystic Assistant, Geffen Fields

'''Required:''' Mystic Job Lv70

'''Objective:''' Control the 4 natural elements and be approved by the Grandmaster.

=== [[File:Kingslayer.gif]]Kingslayer Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Dethroned King, Moscovia

'''Required:''' Duelist Job Lv70

'''Objective:''' Face the usurper and bring justice back to a lost kingdom.

=== [[File:Sinner.gif]]Sinner Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' High Priest Zhed, Rachel Temple Garden

'''Required:''' Deadeye Job Lv70

'''Objective:''' Find the truth about the darkness covering the holy lands.

=== [[File:Blastjuggler.gif]]Blast Juggler Quest ===

'''Start Location:''' Mad Scientist, Lighthalzen

'''Required:''' Saboteur Job Lv70

'''Objective:''' Track down Wolfchev and make sure he pays for his crimes with his life.
