== A True Open World ==
[[File:Instancelist.png|thumb|299px|Instancelist.png]]
Running the same corridor over and over?
Repeating the same cutscenes and same bosses, always in the same order, to no end?
Not Here!
Instances here are a narrative experience. You are not expected to do any of them more than once! And they are usually pretty short experiences, tailor-made to be both interesting, unique and provide additional challenges.
'''No''' farming/grinding content is in instances.

=== Main Story Instances ===
The main story is comprised of 12 Quests. But only 8 of them are instance-based. The final instance also has a party-based version with a harder fight.
All main story rewards are storage-able and cannot be traded.
