The '''Rune System''' is a unique system for Return to Morroc.

Runes have powerful bonuses. These bonuses can be improved by refining the runes. A rune is harder to refine than a common equipment.

The runes also change the color of your character when equipped. So it's also a strategic element, as for example, Wearing a fire damage boosting rune, will change one's color to red.

Runes can be obtained in a variety of ways.

== Rune Types ==
[[File:Whiterune.png|26x26px]] '''Neutral Rune''': Simple runes that can't be refined, can be used at any level and provide minor bonuses, perfect for beginners.

<br/>
[[File:Whiterune.png|26x26px]] '''White Rune''': Runes with unique variations that change the way you can engage content.

[[File:Darkrune.png|26x26px]] '''Dark Rune''': Job Runes. They improve specific skills and stats of the job they belong to. Only one per job is available.

[[File:Purplerune.png|26x26px]] '''Purple Rune''': Power Runes. Improve overall mechanical performance of the wearer.

[[File:Orangerune.png|26x26px]] '''Orange Rune''': Growth Runes. Helps the wearer grow and evolve faster in a variety of ways.

[[File:Greenrune.png|26x26px]] '''Green Rune''': Guard Runes. improves general defensive powers of the wearer.

[[File:Bluerune.png|26x26px]] '''Blue Rune''': Elemental Runes. Boost power and resistances to certain elements.

[[File:Specialrune.png|21x23px]] '''Special Rune''': Unique Runes. provide unique properties that can't be obtained anywhere else.
