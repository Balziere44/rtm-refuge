== Kafra Services ==
The Kafras are a core part of the experience. Over the years, the quality of their work has been expanded beyond their usual facilities.
* Location of most kafras has been changed to more convenient locations.
* Only one kafra is located in each city. usually near the main area.
* Kafras can now teleport players to every city, under specific geographical conditions.
* The orphans have exclusive access to '''Kafra Alysia''' in [[The Orphanage]].

== Regular Kafra Services ==
=== Save Point ===
Saves your return point to the current kafra location. When player dies, they respawn at the save point.

=== Storage ===
Offers access up to 3 storages at a small zeny price [Except for Kafra Alysia] . This price varies by region and kafra location.

=== Teleport ===
Allows teleporting to any main city. Teleport has a fixed price of 1000z to any city in the same region, or 1500z to a capital in another region (Prontera, Yuno or Rachel).
* Regular Kafras cannot teleport to Niflheim, Nomad Village, Mora or Dicastes Diel.

=== Refill ===
Allows refilling the '''Kafra Elixir'''. This has no cost and can be used anytime.

Automatically refils Elixir near Alycia if you are missing any of them.

== Kafra Elixir ==
[[File:Kafraelixir.gif|18px|link=File_Kafraelixir.html]]
The Kafra elixir is a special service provided by any Kafra.
Kafras will refill all elixir of a player when asked. By Default, this amount is '''2'''.
This service is completely free.
* Kafra Elixirs Recover 50% max HP and SP when used.
* Kafra Elixirs have a 5 second cooldown.
* Kafra Elixirs have no weight and can't be traded, stored, sold or discarded.
* Wearing some equipment like an '''Elixir Badge''' or pieces of the '''Ambrosia Shadow Set''' will increase the amount of refills a player can have.
* Removing any equipment that affects kafra elixir amount breaks any remaining elixir the player has.
* Finding Kafra Alysia's Ring and bringing it to her permanently increases the refills -she- provides to '''3'''. This is once per character.

== Kafra Express ==
[[File:Kafraexpress.png|187px|link=File_Kafraexpress.html|right]]
The Kafra Express is a unique part of the experience in Return to Morroc.
These Kafra employees can provide assistance to players in far-away locations.
* They allow access to the storage.
* They sell simple consumables like magnifiers, fly wings and potions.
* They can refill your Kafra Elixir.
* They '''cannot''' save your respawn location.
* They '''cannot''' teleport you.
* '''Kafra Express storage and shop have an increased price.'''
* VIP players can use these services for regular prices.

Kafra Express can be found near the entrance of some dungeons, distant fields and desolated areas. They cannot be found in any distortion.
