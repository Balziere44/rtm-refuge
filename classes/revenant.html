Final job

Revenant

Lady Hel's subject. Counters, leech, and a spirit that stuns.

What the Refuge changed

  • Ominous Presence takes on the overheal mechanic; 3% per level of the extra leech becomes shield.
  • Flaming Wave loses its cast time and burns five times longer.
  • Final Orchestra heals 1% of your max HP per pulse per level, and deals half that as damage plus knockback.

Strengths

Consistent Dps, High leech power, good AoE, inbuilt Leech -> Barrier.

Weaknesses

Weakness to CC, ranged damage, and lingering AoE skills.

Gameplay Identity

The Revenant is a unique DPS class that focuses on a life-steal mechanic combined with a shield generated from excess life force. They are a sustained damage dealer, relying on consistently draining life from their enemies to fuel their own survivability.

Upon job change , Revenants are given pet egg, which can be either:

- Hatched for combat pet, who stuns and burns enemies.

or

- Changed to Revenant Ebel card which gives crit chance/dmg.
