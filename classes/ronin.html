Gameplay Identity

Ronin has high damage potential as long as you keep up buffs and Combo/Finisher Ready status.

Great movement speed and mobility via offensive skills and passives. High flee rate making Ronin unkillable with good preparation.

Highest Perfect dodge among all classes.

Tranquil battlefield

An open, flowing cut breeze

At the Perfect Blade

Core Gameplay

Ronin excels at dealing critical damage. Main passive skill grants huge critical strike chance for hit penalty. All of the ronin skills can crit.

Way of the blade skill grants buff that increases damage of every skill, and also gives ability to "evade" any physical attack or skill.

Instant Draw is heavy combo skill that increases in hits depending on Ready status you have active.

Void dash is unique ranged skill that scales on movement speed, dashing across target and dealing delayed damage.

Ronin Also has unique off-hand scabbard that is given on job change.
