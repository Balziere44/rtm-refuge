Peacekeeper

Whips and trinkets, in one continuous combo.

What the Refuge changed

  • Use Trinket base damage doubled and DEX scaling tripled.
  • Both Andromeda skills hit harder on shorter cooldowns.
  • New gear was added specifically for this job, which was short of it.
  • Chain Mastery and Assault Mode were pulled back to pay for it.

Strengths

High damage potential. High leech power with certain trinkets. High natural resistances. Weapon Endows from trinkets. Damage scaling depends on trinkets used.

Weaknesses

High skill ceiling. Trinkets are expensive to make.Squishy without investments. Long Cooldown on finisher skills.

Gameplay Identity

Peacekeeper is melee dps job that uses whips and scales mainly on DEX instead of STR. Combo playstyle offers high dps with great sustain if you can keep combo going.

Unique trinkets can change or improve your desired playstyle, change your weapon endows and stat scaling on skills, or other unique effects.

Using Assault Mode grants increased offensive stats for exchange in defences. Use with caution.

Gameplay Identity

Peacekeeper has consistent dps while using combo skills. Each damaging skill has 2-3 effects depending on combo state. Giving endless chain of heavy hits. By adding trinktes use on top of active combo can further improve option on damagin potential of the class.

Trinkets are non-consumable throwing items, that grants effects depending on "Use trinket" skil level. Level 1~5 Deals damage depending on trinket. Level 6~10 Deals damage and grants trinket effect while under turnabout mode with increased cooldown.

Possible combo:

Constant DPS combo:

Conviction -> Eternal Chain -> Conviction -> Use Trinket [1~5] -> Conviction -> Repeat.

Finisher combo:

Use Trinket [6~10] -> Conviction -> Eternal Chain -> Conviction -> Lashing Andromeda(Single target) or Wrecking Andromeda(AoE).

AutoAttack combo:

Use Trinket [1~5] -> Autocast Conviction -> Eternal Chain -> Repeat.

Or

Enter Turnabout mode -> Use Trinket [6~10] -> Autocast Conviction -> Eternal Chain -> repeat

And a lot more if you wish to push Peacekeeper even further.

Peacekeeper HQ

In Marrol Kingdom the Free city of Frontier gives access to Peacekeeper HQ upon changing job to Peacekeeper, where you can gain information about "secret" Trinket crafting.

Around the HQ certain Npc can give information about trinkets crafting and materials needed for them.

To craft trinkets you need to enter Place of Power in the middle of the room, while having required materials you can craft every trinket.

Crafting trinket will require 1 refine material and 1 unique catalyst.
