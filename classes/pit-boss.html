Golf Clubs and Chains

What it is

  • Doubles down on the tree's identity: prepare big swings, land big strikes.
  • Most skills have fixed cast time, so gear that reduces it is not optional - it is the build.
  • Golf clubs carry an innate reduction to fixed cast time.
  • Chains raise the job's defensive properties directly, as a percentage of your current defence.
  • Roughly a Dracomancer's HP and slightly more SP.
  • Very flexible, but pulled toward one of two poles: quick, with combos feeding a finisher, or slow, with fixed-cast skills that hurt a great deal when they land.
  • Full skill list on the new jobs page.

This job is new in the Refuge, so there is no inherited documentation for it. Everything here comes from the designer's own posts.
