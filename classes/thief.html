Thief

Path of the hero. Endless skills and spells.

Join the community server Full class tree Skills

What the Refuge changed

  • Improve Defense rebuilt: 2 HP per skill level per base level. Roughly 3,900 HP at level 130, where it used to be a flat 150.
  • Improve Wisdom rebuilt: 2 SP per skill level per 3 base levels, roughly 1,300 SP at level 130.
  • Envenom can poison bosses.
  • Double Strafe, Arrow Shower, Backstab and Cross Ripper Slasher all hit harder.

The Thief is a resourceful fighter, it can wield multiple weapon types and evolve into multiple paths.

Over the years, the Thief guild worked hard not only to expand its skill set to allow multiple new training paths, but to fully utilize the potential of Orphans to achieve greater advances.

Thief no longer has access to Double Attack, Double Attack is an innate bonus depending on the Daggers.

The Thief is a job class that can specialize in any kind of damage in the game. It also can wield multiple weapons to achieve this.

Max Job Level for Thief is 50. A Thief can become either an Assassin, a Rogue or Unchain its limits.

Extra information

  • Lydia is the guild leader for the Thief Guild. She is a very easy-going person and only expects good results from guild members.
  • Available Weapons: Daggers, Swords, Bows
