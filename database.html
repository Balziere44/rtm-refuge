The database

Items, skills, monsters and maps, maintained in the open and built so the people who play can correct it.

What it covers

The head admin has been building a reference database alongside the server itself. It carries tabs for skills, monsters, maps and items, plus fields for discussion - notes on what needs a buff, a nerf or a fix, attached to the thing being discussed rather than lost in a chat channel.

That last part is the point. The plan is a world where everyone can contribute, and the database is the mechanism.

Work in progress. Take numbers with a grain of salt. The server has not launched, the balance lists are still moving, and the database is being filled in as the build settles.
