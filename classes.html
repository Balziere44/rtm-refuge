Every job, rebalanced

Three rebalance passes, transcribed in full. The shared baseline first, then the assassin branch, then the rogue branch. Numbers marked as work in progress may still move before launch.

Read this first. Monsters deal more damage than they did and healing is slower. Most of the damage increases below are paying for that, not raising your ceiling. And the job shadow sets are gone, so the overall power curve is lower than the original's by design.

-

Pass one

The shared baseline and the early game

The skills every character has, then each job in turn.

Orphan (shared baseline)

The skills every character carries. These four changes touch every build in the game.

  • Heal capped at level 1. The formula was rewritten to scale off base level instead of skill level, so it stays relevant instead of falling off. Cooldown 5s to 7s.
  • Hiding capped at level 1, fixed 2 second duration, 5s cooldown, cannot be cancelled early - but now works on insects, demons and bosses.
  • Warp Portal is now a default skill. Memorise three locations plus your save point and warp yourself and the whole party.
  • Teleport level 1 is free for everyone, 60 second cooldown, no SP cost, behaves like a Fly Wing. Level 2 was removed.
  • Increase AGI no longer grants ASPD or AGI. Slightly better move speed instead, 20s + 2s per AGI.
  • Heal, Hiding and Increase AGI now cost 15 SP + 5% of max SP.

Thief line

  • Envenom can poison bosses. Better INT scaling, chance and duration rise with skill level.
  • Improve Defense rebuilt: 2 HP per skill level per base level. About 150 HP before, roughly 3,900 at level 130.
  • Improve Wisdom rebuilt: 2 SP per skill level per 3 base levels. About 150 SP before, roughly 1,300 at level 130.
  • Double Strafe 50 + 7%/lv to 50 + 10%/lv.
  • Arrow Shower combo damage 80% to 100%.
  • Elemental spell bonus per focus stack 6% to 7%.
  • Backstab scaling per Improve Dodge level 3% to 5%.
  • Cross Ripper Slasher damage per counter 20% to 25%.

Assassin

  • Axe Tornado damage per Rolling Cutter counter 15% to 25%.
  • Axe Boomerang base damage 60% to 100%.
  • Sonic Blow LUK scaling 1% to 2% per LUK.
  • Burst Petals base damage 60% to 100%, hit-count bug fixed.
  • Magic Pierce no longer adds 1% def pierce per active level.
  • Cloaking is not detected by normal monsters, but bosses see you.

Rogue

  • Decoy cooldown 5s to 8s, works on non-bosses.
  • Ricochet Arrow base 50% to 75%, combo bonus 70% to 75%.
  • Shield Mastery bonus to shield skills 5%/lv to 10%/lv.
  • Sneak Attack bonus 30% to 20%, still halved against bosses.

Jester

  • Gift Card 70 + 7%/lv to 77 + 7%/lv, cooldown 5s to 3s.
  • Stack Deck proc chance for Joker's Draw is now 5% per 7 LUK instead of a flat 30%.
  • Joker's Draw 70 + 5%/lv to 77 + 7%/lv, and refunds 77 SP on use.
  • Double Down 30 + 10%/lv to 35 + 15%/lv.

Illusionist

  • Life Strings now grants 10% to 20% of the player's stats per level, duration and cooldown 10s to 30s.

Dark Knight

  • Conflagration gains 50% base damage, HP scaling adjusted.
  • Vengeance cooldown 1.4s to 2s, less HP-based scaling.
  • Fatal Menace 150 + 10%/lv + 30% per missing HP to 150 + 15%/lv + 10% per missing HP.
  • Night Menace 150 + 10%/lv + 50% per missing HP to 150 + 20%/lv + 25% per missing HP.
  • Shatter Cross damage against frozen targets 3% to 10% per STR.
  • Knight Ritual now boosts STR and VIT instead of AGI and DEX.

Judge

  • New revolvers added to the pool.
  • Death Sentence 50 + 10%/lv to 100 + 10%/lv, DEX scaling 1% to 3%, combo bonus 50% to 100%.
  • Violent Barrage base 60% to 75%.
  • Gun Swap DEX bonus from combo 2% to 3% per DEX.

Vagabond and Legend

No direct changes - only what flows down from the shared passives such as Improve Defense and Improve Wisdom.

No direct changes.

Raider

  • Improve Defense and Improve Wisdom follow the thief rework.
  • Dragon Thrust base 120% to 110%.
  • Heavy Stab 50 + 30%/lv + 2%/VIT to 100 + 25%/lv + 3%/VIT.
  • Wind Cutter base 40% to 60%.
  • Spear Mastery one-hand spear HP 30 to 25 per level, two-hand spear 2 to 3 flee per level.

Dracomancer

Dragon Orbs are meant to be a strong extra, not a button you press to always transform and stomp everything.

  • Orb cooldowns 60-5s per level to a flat 90s.
  • Orb duration flat 10s to 10 + 2s per level.
  • Sky Prophecy cooldown 15s to 12s, cast time set to 2s.
  • Crescent Dive scaling 15%/lv to 25%/lv.
  • Geirskogul base 80% to 100%; 25% stun chance on dragons became 10% on any enemy.
  • Draco Wings no longer costs 5% max HP.
  • Dragon Breath 25%/lv to 30%/lv, cast time no longer grows with level.

Trickster

  • Hellraiser base 110% to 150%, the level 10 requirement for Finisher Ready is gone, duration 3s to 5s.
  • Sweeping Slash hits three times during Finisher Ready.

Revenant

  • Ominous Presence takes on croc's overheal mechanic; its hit and critical damage were removed. 3% per level of the extra leeched damage becomes shield.
  • Flaming Wave loses its cast time, burn 0.5 + 0.5s/lv to 2.5 + 0.5s/lv, cooldown fixed at 5s, damage 25%/lv to 30%/lv.
  • Final Orchestra flat healing became 1% of the caster's max HP per pulse per skill level, and deals half that as damage plus knockback to anything standing in it. Max SP cost 50% to 25%.

Peacekeeper

  • Chain Mastery ATK per level 3 to 1.
  • Assault Mode DEF/MDEF pierce 5%/lv to 3%/lv.
  • Use Trinket base 50% to 100%, 1% to 3% per DEX, cooldowns 5s to 4s and 15s to 10s for Crash.
  • Lashing Andromeda 50 + 10%/lv + 1%/DEX to 50 + 15%/lv + 2%/DEX, cooldown 10s to 7s.
  • Wrecking Andromeda 50 + 10%/lv + 1%/DEX to 100 + 15%/lv + 2%/DEX, cooldown 10s to 7s.

Ronin

  • Ascending and Descending Steel INT scaling 1% to 2%.
  • Ascending Steel grants Kaupe for 1 second.

Shadowseer

  • Max job level 70 to 50.
  • Magic Pierce removed from the tree.

Prowler

  • Max job level 70 to 50. Nothing else changed.

Pass two

The assassin branch

Specialisations and final jobs.

Blade Dancer / Night Raven

croc's leech and ranged bonuses on the bird skills are kept.

  • Soul Destroyer applies poison to any target including bosses while Enchant Poison is up. 25 + 5%/lv chance, 5 + 1s/lv duration.
  • Counter Slash starting cooldown 0.5s to 0.6s.
  • Night Hunt hit count doubled (20 at level 10), HP loss per hit 5% to 7%, 1% damage per 1% missing HP, 1.25x at red health.
  • Bloody Fangs range 11 to 3 cells, cooldown 4s to 5s, no more double damage with dual axes (folded into a flat 50%/lv + 5%/INT, still MATK based), triple damage at red health, ends Night Hunt early.
  • Cloaking Exceed move speed 90 + 10%/lv to 40%/lv.
  • Typhoon Edge now boosted by dual axes.
  • Definitive Dagger range increased to 3 cells.

Scoundrel / Black Plague

Most toxins affect bosses, carried over from croc's build.

  • Venom Buster base cooldown 10s to 5s.
  • Spiral Blade slightly faster repeat, damage per level 10% to 15%.
  • Mjolnir's Fall damage per level 10% to 25%.
  • Enchant Deadly Poison now negates the entire flat defence rather than 75% of it.

Executioner / Gravekeeper

Animation lock timers were reweighted toward after-cast delay.

  • Meteor Assault +5% damage per Rolling Counter.
  • Exale Rage area doubled to 11x11.
  • Mass Grave cast time removed.
  • Guard Breaker final damage +25% per defensive buff on the target - Kyrie, Assumptio, Auto Guard, Defender, Reflect and friends.
  • Fissure Tooth DEX scaling 2% to 3%.

Shinobi / Satsujin

Honestly the most balanced job in the game, and one of the hardest to play. It did not need changes.

  • Dragon Omamori DEX scaling 2% to 3%.
  • Million Stab cooldown 15s to 7s, since it lost the gear that supported it.

Pass three

The rogue branch

Specialisations and final jobs.

Mystic / Arcane Master

Still one of the hardest jobs to play at full capacity.

  • Dual Cast duration and cooldown 15s to 30s.
  • Unlimited Power duration 30s, cooldown 60s, magic defence pierce 3%/lv to 1%/lv, MATK bonus 3%/lv to 5%/lv. While it is up: every skill in the game loses its cooldown, every skill gains an irreducible 1s cast time, and every skill costs double SP.
  • Comet cooldown 20s to 10s, INT scaling 6% to 7%.

Saboteur / Blast Juggler

Carry weight now scales off base level rather than STR, so hauling materials is easier.

  • Burning Field scaling 1%/lv to 2%/lv, cooldown 5s to 4s, fire bottle cost 3 to 2.
  • Greater Explosion cooldown 10s to 8s, acid bottle cost 3 to 2.
  • Hot Trap and Cold Trap now ignore defence; formula 150 + 5 x lv x INT becomes 100 + 10 x lv x INT.
  • Claymore Trap ignores defence and triples damage against enemies at full HP.
  • Cluster Bomb triples damage against enemies below 10% HP - this one does not ignore defence.
  • Rolling Flames area halved to 3x3, hit detection twice as fast, no longer clears ground effects, scaling 15%/lv to 10%/lv.
  • Fire Expansion passive bonus to Rolling Flames 20%/lv to 15%/lv.
  • Mr. Bombastic INT scaling 3% to 2% per INT, and it remains the ground-clearing tool.

Duelist / Kingslayer

  • Duel Stance: Riposte and Delta Skyfall gain 1% per VIT for every 2 duel counters (5% per VIT at cap).
  • Retribution damage per counter doubled, SP cost 50 to 10.
  • King's Chains weight influence up about 8%, and adds your soft and hard defence as extra final damage. Cooldown 5s to 8s.
  • Queen's Gambit grants 5 duel counters on hit.
  • King's Gambit grants 5 duel counters when it clears anything from the ground.
  • Check Mate rebuilt: was a fixed 400 ATK multiplied by twice your total SP. Now a normal formula - 100 + 3 x current SP + current HP/10 as a percentage of attack.
  • Bishop's Tax 30% (20% on bosses) against afflicted targets became a flat 15% against everything.

Deadeye / Sinner

The Godhand arrow combo window is more forgiving.

  • Sniper Nest fully reworked. It used to trade move speed and flee for damage. Now: -150 flee, +150 hit, Headshot and Aimed Bolt always crit while active, and all SP costs rise 25%.
  • Headshot always deals full damage regardless of Sniper Nest, and its cooldown is halved when cast from the nest. Bonus per DEX during combo 15% to 10%.
  • Aimed Bolt always critical inside Sniper Nest.
  • Cannon Turret receives 7% per level of its owner's ATK, walks with them and attacks the same target.
  • Arrow Storm cooldown 7s to 10s, damage 20%/lv to 25%/lv, DEX scaling 4% to 5%.

Two jobs are missing from this page

Bouncer and Pit Boss did not exist before, so there is nothing to compare them to. They have a page of their own.
