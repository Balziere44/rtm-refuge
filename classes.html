41 classes, seven roads

Everyone starts as an Orphan. Where you go after job level 10 decides how the next hundred and forty levels feel - and two of these roads did not exist before the Refuge.

Job shadow sets are gone. In the original, every job had its own mandatory set past level 100 and it decided your build for you. The Refuge removed them and replaced them with more than fifty dungeon-based sets that any job can wear. Every page below is written with that already true.

Everyone starts here

Ten job levels as an Orphan and the whole tree opens. The Orphan's own skills stay with you for all one hundred and fifty levels, which is why the Refuge rebuilt four of them before touching anything else.

OrphanEveryone starts here. Nobody stays.

The Thief road

The main progression, and the widest. Ten job levels as an Orphan, fifty as a Thief, then a choice that eventually forks eight ways.

ThiefThe main road. Melee, magic and ranged in one tree.
AssassinPath of Tradition. Swords, axes, katars and shadows. RoguePath of Change. Four different characters live in this tree.
Blade DancerMaster of blades. Dual wielding, properly. ShinobiMaster of elements. Change the element, change the fight. ExecutionerMaster of katars. Chain the skills, land the damage. ScoundrelMaster of poisons. Debuff everything, including yourself. MysticSpellcasting, unlocked. DuelistA stance, counter charges, and skills that read them. SaboteurTraps, explosions, and a pitcher for the party. DeadeyeThe bow, the nest, and one very heavy finisher.
Night RavenEvery assassin weapon, plus a hawk that never misses. SatsujinMelee, magic and ranged trees at once. Jack of all trades. GravekeeperLoki's legacy. Crit damage, and no defensive options at all. Black PlagueDouble axes and diseases. Kills bosses from the inside. Arcane MasterUnrestricted magic, plus a tree that revives the party. KingslayerChess. A left tree that kills, a right tree that survives. Blast JugglerRules? Explosions are the only option. SinnerRepent through cleansing. Auto attack, turret or manual cast.

Leaving the road

Thieves can abandon the path entirely. Assassins and Rogues can lock their job forever instead of specialising.

ProwlerAbandon the path. Flee, perfect dodge, and a better Backstab. ShadowseerMelee that spends magic. Autocast until something dies.
Unchained ThiefThe Super Novice of this world. Core skills from both second jobs. Unchained AssassinBreak the limit, lock the job, master the basics. Unchained RogueBreak the limit, lock the job, master the basics.
Phantom ThiefInfinite possibilities. Most specialisation skills, and every job's shadow set.

The Raider road

Fast job levels, harsh job quests, and a trial nobody will describe.

RaiderSpears, staying power, and a trial that ends in a dragon.
DracomancerDraco Orbs instead of runes, and a dragon you can borrow.

The unbound

Trickster and Vagabond ignore their destiny in opposite directions. Seventy job levels each before the final change.

TricksterScythes, innate leech, and a three-step combo you never stop pressing. VagabondDaggers and auto attacks. No rotation, no excuses.
RevenantLady Hel's subject. Counters, leech, and a spirit that stuns. LegendDual wielding, and passives on top of passives.

Standing alone

Three jobs with no second step. Jester breaks the story, Ronin meditates on it, Mimic laughs at it.

JesterA deck, a huge SP pool, and the highest attack speed ceiling in the game. RoninEvery skill can crit. Every hit taken takes your buffs with it. MimicEvery skill in the game, if the dice agree.

The Expert jobs

Unique systems, steeper learning curves, and explicitly not recommended for a first character.

JudgeRevolvers you craft yourself, and 100 job levels to fill. PeacekeeperWhips and trinkets, in one continuous combo. IllusionistTwo characters in one. Yours, and Kimi's. Dark KnightThe lower your health, the harder you hit.

New in the Refuge

Two jobs that did not exist before. Same progression timing as Raider to Dracomancer, so the second step comes quickly.

Bouncer NewFists, punch combos, and the first casting skills in the tree.
Pit Boss NewBig setup, big hits. Golf clubs and chains.

Not sure where to start?

Thief is the main road and the most forgiving. Ronin, Jester and the Expert jobs are explicitly not recommended for a first character.
