42 classes, seven roads

Everyone starts as an Orphan. Where you go after job level 10 decides how the next hundred and forty levels feel - and two of these roads did not exist before the Refuge.

Job shadow sets are gone. In the original, every job had its own mandatory set past level 100 and it decided your build for you. The Refuge removed them and replaced them with more than fifty dungeon-based sets that any job can wear. Every page below is written with that already true.

Everyone starts here

Ten job levels as an Orphan and the whole tree opens. The Orphan's own skills stay with you for all one hundred and fifty levels, which is why the Refuge rebuilt four of them before touching anything else.

OrphanCursed by fate. The starter, and the only one.

Straight from the Orphan

Ten job levels and you are one of these. None of them changes again - what you pick here is what you play for a hundred and forty levels.

JesterPlays with destiny. A barrage of skills. RoninPerfect play. Every skill can crit, every hit taken costs you. MimicEvery skill in the game, if the dice agree. Merchant NewA life of peace and profits. Cannot fight, can sell.

One more step

Four roads with exactly two stations. The first job comes at job level 10, the second at 70 for Trickster and Vagabond, sooner for Raider and Bouncer.

RaiderThe forgotten myth. Heart of weapons. TricksterNiflheim's eyes in Midgard. Lifesteal, criticals and fire. VagabondForging your destiny. Auto attack specialist. Bouncer NewFists, punch combos, and the first casting skills in the tree.
DracomancerThe storm walker. Bound to dragons. RevenantMaster of life and death. Engagement and explosive damage. LegendMaster of your destiny. Battlefield dominance with power. Pit Boss NewBig setup for big hits.

The Expert jobs

Unique systems, steeper learning curves, and explicitly not recommended for a first character. Base level 15 and job level 10 to enter.

JudgePower from the royalty. High preparation for high burst. PeacekeeperWhips and trinkets, in one continuous combo. IllusionistMemories given life. Summons and illusion spells. Dark KnightVengeance incarnate. Life energy into power.

The Thief road

The main progression, and the widest. Fifty job levels as a Thief, then a choice that eventually forks eight ways.

ThiefPath of the hero. Endless skills and spells.
AssassinPower of tradition. Master of weapon arts. Unchained ThiefPower of tradition and change. User of multiple schools. RoguePower of change. Jack of all trades.

Leaving the road

A Thief can abandon the path outright. An Assassin or a Rogue can lock the job forever instead of specialising.

ShadowseerDuality of reality. A rain of autospells. ProwlerEvasive backstabber. Close in and slaughter.
Unchained AssassinBreak the limit and lock the job. Master of the basic arts. Unchained RogueBreak the limit and lock the job. Master of the basic arts. Phantom ThiefMaster of tradition and change. Infinite possibilities.

Path of Tradition

Assassin specialisations lift the job level cap from 50 to 70 and are required for a final job.

Blade DancerMaster of blades. Dual wielding, properly. ShinobiMaster of elements. Change the element, change the fight. ScoundrelMaster of poisons. Debuff everything, including yourself. ExecutionerMaster of katars. Chain the skills, land the damage.
Night RavenUnseen blade from the skies. Speed, mobility, precision. SatsujinMaster of shadows and magic. Attacks from shadows and spells. Black PlagueUnseen death from inside. Poisons, toxins, curses and death. GravekeeperLoki's legacy for Midgard. Critical attacks and combos.

Path of Change

Rogue specialisations, same rules. Four of the most different final jobs in the game come out of this row.

DuelistA stance, counter charges, and skills that read them. MysticSpellcasting, unlocked. SaboteurTraps, explosions, and a pitcher for the party. DeadeyeThe bow, the nest, and one very heavy finisher.
KingslayerMaster of strategy and war. Versatility, control, protection. Arcane MasterUnrestricted forbidden magic. Spellcasting, autospells, elements. Blast JugglerNo restrictions or rules. Bombs, traps and explosions. SinnerRepent through cleansing. Smiting arrows and turrets.

Not sure where to start?

Thief is the main road and the most forgiving. Ronin, Jester and the Expert jobs are explicitly not recommended for a first character.
