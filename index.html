The short version

A better version of the thing we already loved

The original ran for years and then stopped. Its developer came back, another developer came with him, and an admin paid for the hardware. None of them are trying to build a business. They are trying to build a place worth logging into.

What changed

Everything that was wrong, on a list

Transformed, added, removed - the developers' own three buckets, written out in full and kept up to date.

 Classes

Every job, rebalanced

Line by line. The skills nobody used got attention first, and the shared baseline skills were rebuilt from scratch.

 New

Bouncer and Pit Boss

An ultra-aggressive melee caster with a brand new weapon type. Big setup, big hits, and yes, one of the skills is a Swanton Bomb.

 World

21 distortions and three SS dungeons

Levels 85 to 150, ranked B to SS, with where to find each one - plus the new endgame the team has been building since June.

 Guides

The community's own writing

Hidden quests, dungeon access, costume hunts. Written by players, collected here so nobody has to dig through chat history.

 Reference

The database

Items, skills, monsters and maps, maintained by the admin and open for the community to correct. Work in progress.

Three promises

What this place will not do to you

Nothing is for sale

No cash shop at launch. No real-money trading, ever. The admin covered the costs himself and has said any future discussion about recouping them happens in the open, with the community, and never touches power.

Your time is the budget

The experience rate is slightly lower than the original's, and the curve is more rewarding for it. Warp Portal and Teleport are free for everyone. Drop-rate gear is gone, so nobody is farming for the right to farm.

No single correct build

The job-locked shadow sets that funnelled everyone into one playstyle past level 100 are gone. The replacement sets exist to make the neglected half of each skill tree worth pressing.

How it got here

From a poll to three finished dungeons

  1. 19 April 2026

    The call

    Metta reaches out after a poll on another project's server. Instead of a new server, he wants the old one back - and better. The project starts that day.

  2. 4 May 2026

    Doors open

    The community server goes public. No launch date, an open invitation to ask anything, and a promise: this is not a professional operation, it is a good place to play.

  3. 7-14 May 2026

    The rebalance posts

    Job-by-job change lists go up for the early game, the assassin branch and the rogue branch. Job shadow sets are confirmed gone.

  4. 14 May 2026

    Two new jobs

    Bouncer and Pit Boss are announced. A first job and a final job, fast progression between them, and a weapon type that did not exist before.

  5. 7 June 2026

    Bouncer plays

    Gameplay footage. Tanky, versatile, flexible - and it will drink your SP.

  6. 27 June 2026

    Pit Boss design

    The full skill tree, the golf clubs, the chains, and the reasoning behind fixed cast times.

  7. 5 July 2026

    Nightmare in Amatsu

    The SS dungeon is revealed. Level 150 recommended, no minimap, and a random spawn point.

  8. 1 August 2026

    Two more SS dungeons

    Both finished, both on fully custom maps. One of them stays secret.

Dates come from the team's own announcements. The full story, including who does what, is on The Server.
